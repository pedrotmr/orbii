# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who have tried traditional habit trackers with long daily checklists (often 8–15 habits), felt guilt or failure when they could not do everything every day, and still want those habits in their life — just not as a permanent all-or-nothing scoreboard.

They open Orbii in short daily moments (often morning), wanting a manageable focus set, not another productivity dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Orbii separates “habits I want to keep in my life” from “what deserves my attention today.”

Users maintain a personal Orbit of habits they care about. Each day, Orbii surfaces a small Today’s Orbit. Completing that set means the day succeeded — even if many other habits stay in orbit unused that day.

Rotation is the product. Random or simple selection is fine for v1; smarter scheduling can come later. The emotional goal is manageable, positive, low-pressure completion (“Today’s Orbit complete”), not coverage guilt (“3/10 done”).

Success looks like: users keep a richer habit set than a 2–3 item tracker, without the burnout of a full daily checklist — and choose Orbii because the philosophy matches how they actually live.

## Brand Personality

Playful, minimal, optimistic — lightly game-like, never childish.

Voice: clear, warm, slightly surprising. Not corporate productivity. Not guru wellness. Not caveman gimmick.

Craft reference: grug’s daily-ritual confidence and delight (one clear moment, personality in craft, no feature sprawl) — without copying caveman voice or hand-drawn sketch UI.

Emotional goal on open: “I wonder what’s in my Orbit today.”

## Anti-references

- Traditional habit trackers that score incomplete days as failure
- Corporate productivity software aesthetics
- Stereotypical wellness apps (pastel gradients, lotus, motivational clichés)
- Literal space / planet / galaxy app clichés just because the name is Orbii
- Dashboard-first UIs that bury today’s focus under stats and settings
- Gaudy gamification, neon glow stacks, manipulative streak panic

## Design Principles

1. **Orbit ≠ checklist** — Many habits can live in the Orbit; only a few come into focus each day.
2. **Completion beats coverage** — “Today’s Orbit complete” is the win, not a fraction of the full Orbit.
3. **Rotation is the product** — Selection mechanism can start simple; the philosophy must stay visible in every screen.
4. **Pressure stays low** — Skip, swap, or soften a day without shame mechanics.
5. **Gamification serves the ritual** — Streaks/XP only if they reinforce manageable completion; they must never become the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive states.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; orbit/reveal motion must have a calm non-animated equivalent.
- Do not rely on color alone for completion, selection, or error state.
- Touch targets and copy must work for one-handed, short daily sessions.
